# tf-activity-benchmark-audit

An audit of what transcription-factor activity benchmarks actually measure, and a protocol for
benchmarking them honestly. Started from "should I build something beyond ULM?" and ran the gap
check first; the gap check became the result.

**Start with [`writeup.md`](writeup.md)** — or `writeup.html` for the version with figures.
The short version: on knockTF the perturbed TF's own log fold change *alone* recovers it in 98% of
experiments against 45% for ULM+CollecTRI, the whole channel is one autoregulatory edge, its width
scales as 1/sqrt(regulon size), and the corrected baseline is 34% against a 12% per-TF base rate.

## Layout

- `analysis/` scripts, run from inside that directory with `uv run python <script>`. Each is self-contained; later ones import earlier ones and rerun them.
- `data/` knockTF (`knocktf.h5ad`) and CollecTRI (`collectri.parquet`) as downloaded by decoupler. Gitignored.
- `refs/` annotated reference list (`refs/README.md`), fetched full texts, and the Ariadne citation graph.

## What was found (Sept 17-18, 2026)

ULM+CollecTRI on 279 knockTF knockdowns puts the true TF in the top 10% of scores 45% of the time. Stratifying the misses:

- Regulon overlap between TFs does not explain them (`gapcheck.py`). Penalised joint fitting has nothing to fix.
- Huber-loss ULM is worse on unmasked knockTF, 32% vs 45% (`robust_ulm.py`), but about half to two thirds of that is the benchmark, not the method (`masked_huber.py`, F-008/V-008): Huber gives the perturbed TF's own gene, a roughly 5 sd outlier, a median weight of 0.09, so it never collects the self-edge leak that ULM does (masking costs ULM 33 hits and Huber 5). With that gene masked it is 30% vs 34%: still worse on paired rank (p = 0.002) and top-5%, not distinguishable on top-10%. The deficit exists only for TFs with a self-edge; for the 82 experiments without one Huber equals ULM (20/82 each). Any outlier-robust or rank-based scorer will look worse than ULM on unmasked knockTF for this reason alone.
- Propagating scores along TF→TF edges reaches 57% against about 31% on a degree-preserving shuffled graph (`propagate.py`, `controls.py`; the shuffle as first coded was not degree-preserving, see V-004 and tasks.md T-001, the corrected 10-seed rerun gave the same conclusion), but masking the perturbed TF's own gene before ULM erases the gain entirely and leaves propagation below masked ULM, 28% vs 34% (`leak.py`). The edges were carrying the knockdown's own mRNA drop, mostly (84-90% of the lost hits) via upstream regulators' regulons (`selfedge.py`, F-010/V-010): "up sum" propagation algebraically adds the TF's own logFC once per upstream regulator. The cleanest evidence is the 82 experiments whose TF has no self-edge: propagation falls 34% to 20% masked while ULM stays at 24%. Masking instead a co-regulated gene with a comparably negative logFC costs propagation 0.3 points where the TF's own value costs 29 (`coregmask.py`, F-012/V-012).
- Adding the TF's own log fold change to ULM reaches up to 94% (weight 2; 77% at weight 1, 66% at 0.5; the own log fold change alone scores 98%). ULM itself drops from 45% to 34% when that gene is masked, and that drop is entirely the autoregulatory self-edge: deleting only the perturbed TF's own X->X edge gives the same 96/279 hits as masking (F-010). About a quarter of ULM's hits (net 30 of 126) need that edge; about half for self-edge TFs with under ~60 targets, none for regulons of several hundred. The direction follows the edge's sign label (`selfsign.py`, F-011/V-011, in knockTF scored with CollecTRI): removing a +1 self-edge worsens the rank for 81 of 84 TFs (98 to 65 hits of 176 experiments), removing a -1 self-edge improves it for 11 of 12 TFs (8 to 11 of 21; the count is too small to be a rate). CollecTRI assigns +1 to edges with no sign information, so +1 is not "positive autoregulator": the network's `sign_decision` column says 58% of all +1 edges and 80% of +1 self-edges are that default fill, and 92 of the 176 knockTF experiments with a +1 self-edge rest on one (F-016/V-016). Whether the edge is abundance artefact or real autoregulatory signal is not settled by this. All rates in this file are on the 279 of knockTF's 388 experiments whose TF gets a ULM score; of the other 109, 90 have a TF that is not in CollecTRI and 19 a TF with fewer than 5 CollecTRI targets (F-013).

On the 2026 benchmark's one public dataset (TP53, 617 cells) their own pipeline, rerun unpatched, loses 0.0001 AUROC when TP53's own gene is masked — because the direct own-mRNA channel scales as 1/sqrt(regulon size) and TP53 has 772 measured targets whose own transcript is at chance (0.493) between the arms. The direct leak is regime-dependent, not absent: masking each of their 613 scored TFs in turn gives log10|dscore| = -0.53 - 0.46*log10(size), R2 0.80, so it is worth about 1e-2 AUROC at 7 targets and 1e-3 at 300 (`bench2026.py`, F-027/V-027). Their own preprocessing keeps a TF only if its mean drops in the perturbed arm, so that benchmark selects positives on the own-abundance change exactly as decoupler's knockTF filter does. Separately, on that dataset a shape-matched random regulon scores 0.47 +/- 0.14 (central 95% [0.225, 0.762], 100 seeds), a band covering 34 of their 60 published columns for that TF including their own random control.

Conclusion: knockTF-style benchmarks reward abundance detection, not activity inference. That is a statement about the benchmarks, not about ULM: about a quarter of ULM's hits here come through the autoregulatory self-edge, and the other three quarters still beat the per-TF base rate about threefold (F-010, V-022, V-025). The 34% masked figure is the real ULM baseline, against a per-TF base rate of 12%. The margin is regulon-specific: a degree-preserving shuffle gives 7-15% and no own-vs-mate gap. But it is not all TF identity. Inside the same GEO series the same TF already reaches 22% when a DIFFERENT TF was perturbed, and requiring it to beat its own study-mates costs 2 to 8 points (0.344 -> 0.325 lenient, 0.260 strict) on the 77 of 279 experiments where that test is possible. The within-study TF-specific margin is +0.056 (95% study-clustered CI [-0.016, +0.138]; +0.066 with every perturbed TF's gene masked in every row, whose lower bound is a knife-edge and must not be cited as excluding 0). Inside a GEO series a TF is top-10% in 22% of its series-mates' experiments against 14% in same-cell-line experiments from other series (+0.073 [+0.019, +0.178], 106 of 643 scored, F-028/V-028). That lift is specific to the TF's real regulon — a degree-preserving shuffle leaves 6%, a random-judged-TF placebo gives p = 0.003 — and it is not regulon overlap with the series' other perturbed TF. It is two thirds of the perturbed-TF effect (ratio 0.68, 95% [0.21, 1.60]), it lives in small multi-TF series and is absent in knockTF's one 78-TF screen, and series-mean centering removes it at a cost of 0.036 on the baseline. Anyone benchmarking on knockTF should study-control or centre by series. knockTF has 155 such experiments, enough to detect +0.11 and not +0.05, so that margin is unresolved, not small (F-026/V-026). The 2026 Perturb-seq TF-activity benchmark (`refs/`) selects perturbations by the sign of the TF's own mRNA change and does not mask that gene (code, shipped data and Table S1 agree, V-009). Whether its results are inflated by it is untested: under ULM its headline per-TF AUROC can only see the TF's own mRNA through a self-edge, a narrower channel than rank-among-TFs here (tasks.md T-010).

## Where this points

Activity-vs-abundance decoupling: per TF, a slope calibrated on knockdown data (where abundance is the only cause) predicts the abundance-driven activity, and the residual is the post-translational component. Still a weighted mean plus a subtraction. Validate on decoupler's TGF-beta hepatic stellate set (SMAD2/3 should show in the residual channel only) and the COVID/PBMC single-cell sets (STAT1, NF-kB).

Status of this direction (F-015/V-015, F-018/V-018, 2026-09-18): knockTF cannot calibrate that slope, and the direction is untested rather than refuted. decoupler's knockTF keeps only experiments whose TF's own logFC is below -1, so every experiment is a deep knockdown (median -1.74), there is no data between 0 and -1 where the method would be applied, and only about 20 TFs have more than 1 logFC of within-TF dose range. A per-TF slope through the origin of the size first proposed (about 1 ULM t-unit per logFC) is rejected: the design had about 70% power to see it and the within-TF slope is 0.08 (90% CI -0.25 to +0.42, `slopecheck.py`). A within-TF slope near 0.5 can be neither detected nor excluded. A constant per-TF knockdown offset fits knockdowns better but is not an abundance model, since it predicts an effect at zero knockdown. Judging the direction needs a dose-resolved calibration set with own logFC spanning 0 to -3 within TF (Replogle 2022 guide-level efficiency, X-Atlas/Orion; see `refs/README.md`). But the filter is decoupler's default, not knockTF's: `dc.ds.knocktf(thr_fc=None)` returns 907 experiments over 456 TFs (`data/knocktf_full.h5ad`), 643 of them scoreable. On that set (`fulldose.py`, F-022/V-022) a within-TF dose-response exists and is regulon-specific: pooled slope 0.46-0.48 ULM t-units per logFC (95% CI about 0.15 to 0.75), within-TF Spearman +0.17, zero under a permuted-logFC placebo and a shuffled network, not explained by knockdown method or platform. Its size is uncertain (0.2 to 0.7; carried by about ten TFs; median per-TF slope 0.27 with a CI including 0; own logFC is a noisy dose, so the true slope could be near 1), and calibrating it buys nothing out of sample (leave-one-out gain 0.035 RMSE, CI includes 0). knockTF has no measured zero dose: an exact 0.0 in the matrix means the gene was not measured, and small values sit inside the experiment's noise, so the full set is not a source of negative controls. Where the TF's mRNA measurably rose (18 experiments) the regulon score is positive and the TF is not recovered, which is what abundance-coupled activity predicts. So the decoupling direction is still untested, and knockTF cannot test it; that needs a dose measured with its error (Replogle 2022 guide-level data, or knockTF's GEO series reprocessed with per-gene standard errors). Direction decision pending.
